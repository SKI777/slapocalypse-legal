Slapocalypse: Uncensored Tour

A mobile game by ROK77

Slapocalypse: Uncensored Tour is a comedy lane runner for adults. You swipe to change lanes, tap to slap the annoying people in your way, and stay ahead of the angry crowd chasing you. Your character trash-talks the whole way, with the heavy profanity bleeped. The game is played offline, needs no account and shows no ads.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what Slapocalypse: Uncensored Tour ("the game") does with information. "We" and "us" mean the developer, ROK77.

1. Summary

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal information. The game has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics of its own, no in-app purchases and no online features. It works offline.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember things between sessions, the game saves a small amount of information only on your own device:

  • your settings (subtitles, frame rate, swipe sensitivity, graphics quality and volume levels);
  • that you accepted the first-launch content notice (only the notice version, never your age or birth date);
  • your game progress (play totals, unlocked characters and the selected character);
  • which voice lines played recently, so the same joke does not repeat too soon.

This information is not sent to us and we cannot see it. It contains no name, email, contacts, photos, location or messages.

3. Technical data collected by the game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine by Unity Technologies. Unity may automatically collect limited technical information about the device and the app (for example the device model, operating system version, hardware details and engine performance or error information) to operate and improve its engine. This information goes to Unity, not to us. We do not use it to identify you, for advertising or for tracking. Unity's handling of this data is described in the Unity Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions

The game does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files, and it does not read your device's advertising identifier.

5. Advertising, tracking and third parties

The game shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other apps or websites. We do not share or sell any information to third parties.

Some rows in the game's "About & legal" screen open links outside the game: this page opens in your web browser, and "Email support" opens your mail app. The game sends nothing with these links; what your browser or mail app does is covered by its own privacy terms.

6. Children

The game is for mature audiences only. It contains frequent bleeped profanity, some unbleeped mild swearing, crude humor and cartoon slapstick violence. It is made for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. On first launch the game asks players to confirm that they are 18 or older; this is a self-declaration, not age verification. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

7. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you and to fix the problem you report. We do not add you to any mailing list.

8. Retention and deletion

  • Data on your device: it stays until you delete it. In the game, open Settings > About & legal > Reset local data to delete your settings, progress and unlocked characters. Uninstalling the game also deletes all of it.
  • Support emails: we keep them only as long as needed to answer and resolve your request, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU and UK GDPR or Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law, KVKK), you may have the right to ask whether we hold personal data about you, to access, correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its use, and to receive a copy of it. Because the game does not send us personal data, the only personal data we may hold is what you sent us by email. To use any of these rights, write to us at the support address in the Support section; we will answer within the time the law requires. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Future changes to the game

If online features such as accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, multiplayer, ads, analytics or in-app purchases are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released and, where the law requires it, the game will ask for your consent first.

11. Changes to this policy

We post every change on this page and update the effective date above.
